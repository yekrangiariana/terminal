## Scenes — Living Simulations

Scenes are animations that **evolve over time** — unlike patterns, which repeat mathematically, scenes have memory. Fire flickers unpredictably, rain falls, cells grow. Every frame builds on the last.

The best part: all other features (**Transforms, Symmetry, Layer B, Characters, Colours**) work exactly the same with scenes. A simulation of fire through Radial 8 symmetry becomes a mandala of flames.

### How to Use Scenes

1. Open the **Scenes** section and click any scene button
2. Adjust the scene-specific sliders that appear
3. Add **Symmetry**, **Transforms**, or **Layer B** to enhance
4. Click **Exit Scene** to return to math patterns

### Built-In Scenes

**Cell Division** — Cells split 1→2→4→…→32, then restart. Organic, biological feel. Try with slow speed for a meditative dividing-cells effect.

**Particle Rain** — Falling particles with trailing streaks. Adjust wind for diagonal rain. Layer with Tunnel (Multiply) for rain falling into a void.

**Crystal Growth** — Branching structures grow outward with rotational symmetry. **Best scene for mandalas** — try Radial 6 or 8 symmetry for living snowflakes.

**Game of Life** — Conway's classic cellular automaton with ghost trails. Self-organising patterns that constantly surprise. Add turbulence for organic, cell-culture textures.

**Ripples** — Expanding rings from random splash points. Looks like rain on still water. Layer with Circular pattern for complex wave interference.

**Flocking** — Boids that swarm, separate, and flock together. Adjust cohesion vs separation for different behaviours — tight schools of fish or scattered fireflies.

**Wave Propagation** — 2D wave physics with random disturbances. Looks like a vibrating membrane or water surface. Add Radial 4 symmetry for standing-wave patterns.

**Langton's Ant** — Simple rules, complex emergent patterns. Multiple ants with different rule variants (RL, RLR, LLRR, RLLR) create roads, highways, and unexpected symmetries.

**Fire** — Heat rises from the bottom with random sparks. The classic demo effect. Layer a Tunnel on top (Multiply) for fire with depth.

**Maze Generator** — Watches a maze carve itself out in real time with a glowing trail. Mesmerising to watch. Try with X-axis symmetry for mirrored mazes.

**DLA Snowflake** — Particles drift and stick, building fractal crystal structures. With Radial 6 or 8 symmetry, produces spectacular mandala snowflakes.

### Scene + Feature Recipes

- **Crystal Growth + Radial 8** → living mandala that grows itself
- **Fire + Tunnel (Layer B, Multiply)** → flames receding into infinite depth
- **Game of Life + Turbulence 1.5** → biological, cell-culture texture
- **DLA + Radial 6 + blue/white palette** → ice crystal mandala
- **Flocking + Radial 4 + low cohesion** → scattered particle mandala
- **Ripples + Circular (Layer B, Add)** → complex overlapping wave patterns
- **Any scene + different charset** → completely different aesthetic from the same simulation

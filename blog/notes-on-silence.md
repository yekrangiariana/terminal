I have been thinking about silence as a practice rather than an absence.

Most of what passes for quiet in my life is just a reduction in noise —
a slightly emptier inbox, a walk without headphones. Real silence is
something different. It has weight.

## Silence and Systems

In journalism, there is a version of this that matters professionally.
The silence between sources. The gap where a document should exist
but doesn’t. The quote that was never given.

Learning to read those silences is its own skill. Systems produce
them deliberately. Governments, corporations, institutions — they
are often more eloquent in what they withhold than what they say.

## Digital Silence

Online, silence has been almost completely eliminated as a state.

- Notifications colonise the gaps
- Scroll mechanics remove natural stopping points
- The expectation of response compresses thinking time

This isn’t neutral. Thought that has no room to breathe produces
a particular kind of output: reactive, surface-level, confident.

## Finding It Again

I don’t have a solution. I’m suspicious of people who do.

But I think the first step is treating silence as something to
seek rather than something that happens when everything else
has failed.
